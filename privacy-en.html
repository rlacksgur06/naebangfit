Privacy Policy

Naebangfit (내방핏) · Effective November 1, 2026 · 한국어

Naebangfit (“the service”) is a free web app that checks whether a sim racing rig fits your room before you buy one. Every feature works without signing in, and the room and rig you work on stay in this browser only. This policy explains how personal information is handled for sign-in, My Library, the setup gallery, share links and anonymous usage stats. The Korean version is the governing text.

2. How long we keep it

  • Account details and saved setups: until you delete your account, then deleted right away.
  • Sign-in logs: kept briefly under the sign-in provider’s (Supabase’s) policy, then deleted automatically.
  • Setup gallery: kept until the poster deletes it or deletes their account, then deleted right away, together with its reports.
  • Share links: kept with no end date so the people you sent them to can open them any time. To have a link deleted, email it to us at the address below.
  • Anonymous usage stats: up to one year from collection.
  • Deleted files are erased in a way that cannot be recovered.

3. Sharing with third parties

We do not give personal information to other companies or people, except when the law requires it (for example, a request from an investigative authority).

5. Your rights

  • You can view, edit and delete your saved setups in My Library.
  • You can delete your gallery posts at any time in Setup gallery › Mine.
  • “Delete account” at the bottom of My Library deletes your account, all saved setups and your gallery posts right away.
  • For any other request to access, correct, delete or stop processing your information, email us at the address below. We will act on it without delay.

6. Browser storage

We store your sign-in, the room you are working on, display and sound settings, your language and the random ID for usage stats in browser storage (localStorage). We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies. You can clear it at any time by deleting this site’s data in your browser.

7. Security

  • Saved setups are protected row by row in the database so only you can read and change them.
  • Share links open only with a hard-to-guess 8-character code and cannot be listed.
  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • No administrator keys are included in the app.

8. Children under 14

The service does not accept sign-ups from children under 14. If you are under 14, please use it without signing in.
